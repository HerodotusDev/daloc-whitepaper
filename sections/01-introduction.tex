\section{Introduction}

On-chain lending totals nearly \$28 billion in active loans as of mid-2026~\cite{defillama}, yet the dominant pool-based designs~\cite{aave2022,compound2019} serve only variable-rate, open-term use cases. Fixed terms, fixed rates, per-position isolation, and cross-chain collateral lie outside their scope. A holder of yield-bearing assets on one chain who wants leveraged exposure on another must manually compose across protocols, bearing bridging and counterparty risk at each step.

Existing protocols address fragments of the problem. Morpho Blue offers isolated markets on a single chain~\cite{morphoblue2023}. Euler~v2 adds modular vaults with governor-controlled risk~\cite{eulerv2}. Midnight introduces fixed-maturity terms but operates per-market, per-chain~\cite{midnight2025}. Cross-venue prime brokerages aggregate positions under unified margin, gaining netting efficiency but coupling all venue exposure into one solvency computation---a single venue failure can trigger global liquidation.

No existing system simultaneously aggregates liquidity across venues, converts variable funding to fixed terms, isolates per-position risk, and orchestrates cross-chain collateral without trusted bridges. Pool designs also lock capital from the moment of deposit; \daloc{}'s externally-sourced path commits only hedging capital (3--7\% of principal), achieving 15--30$\times$ leverage versus 1:1 direct lending. Institutional participants face a further gap: permissionless order books offer no compliance filtering without fragmenting into separate permissioned forks.

\daloc{} addresses all four requirements on a single platform layer: a unified order book with epoch-based ZK settlement, a Harvest Vault funding loans directly or via external venues, atomic hook chains for multi-step origination, and pluggable zero-knowledge programs that define market behavior. Three products sit on top. \textbf{Borrow} provides fixed-rate, fixed-term loans with cross-chain collateral. \textbf{Prime Trade} extends leveraged credit lines for perpetual trading on Hyperliquid while preserving native yield. \textbf{Earn} gives liquidity providers passive fixed-rate returns under a proof-enforced vault mandate.
