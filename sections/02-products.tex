\section{Products}

\subsection{Borrow}

Borrow lets a user lock collateral on one chain and receive a fixed-rate, fixed-term loan on another. Rate, fee, and total repayment are quoted upfront and locked for the life of the loan. Collateral stays on its home chain and continues accruing native yield---no bridging or unstaking required.

\subsection{Prime Trade}

Prime Trade is \daloc{}'s prime brokerage product: credit origination for holders of yield-bearing assets on Ethereum, Arbitrum, or Base who want perpetual exposure on Hyperliquid~\cite{hyperliquid} without giving up the underlying.

The conventional path requires unstaking, bridging, and depositing---giving up yield and taking on bridging risk. Prime Trade keeps the collateral where it is: the borrower pledges it and the platform opens a fixed-rate credit line routed directly to the trading venue. The borrower never holds the borrowed capital; protocol-controlled margin agents restrict funds to perpetual trading only---withdrawals, spot transfers, and external lending are blocked. \daloc{} takes no directional exposure; it earns from lending spread and builder fees.

\subsection{Earn}

Earn gives liquidity providers passive fixed-rate yield. Capital deposited into the Harvest Vault is deployed into loans by an operator whose strategy is bounded by a publicly auditable mandate, verified per-epoch by zero-knowledge proof (Section~\ref{sec:lp}).
