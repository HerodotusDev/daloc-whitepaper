\section{The Harvest Vault}
\label{sec:lp}

The Harvest Vault's operator originates loans, responds to lifecycle events, and manages portfolio concentration. All actions are bounded by a publicly auditable mandate and enforced per-epoch by a zero-knowledge proof. The vault is the canonical lending book: when it has sufficient idle capital, it funds loans directly at the full fixed rate (Section~\ref{sec:capital}); when demand exceeds capacity, it routes through external venues and commits only hedging reserves. The mandate, not operator reputation, is the trust anchor for deployed capital.

\subsection{Operator and Mandate}

The operator deploys vault capital by submitting solver quotes to the order book (Section~\ref{sec:architecture}), competing on terms like any other solver. The operator's strategy is bounded by a \textbf{mandate}: a policy committed on-chain as a hash at vault deployment. Each mandate specifies:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Allowed collateral set:} asset addresses the operator may accept as loan collateral.
  \item \textbf{Per-collateral LTV ceiling:} maximum loan-to-value ratio the operator may offer on each collateral type.
  \item \textbf{Per-tenor rate floor:} minimum annualized rate the operator must charge, ensuring the vault never underwrites below a specified spread.
  \item \textbf{Portfolio concentration caps:} maximum exposure per borrower, per collateral type, and per market as a fraction of vault NAV.
  \item \textbf{Liquidation reserve requirement:} minimum idle capital maintained at all times for liquidation response and withdrawal settlement.
\end{itemize}

A mandate constrains what the operator \emph{can} do, not what they \emph{must} do; within its bounds, the operator retains full discretion on timing, counterparty selection, and position sizing.

\subsection{Compliance Proof}

Each epoch, the operator produces a zero-knowledge proof that every action satisfied the mandate. The proof takes prior vault state, the epoch's actions, and the mandate hash as public inputs, outputting a compliance fact registered in the facts registry (Section~\ref{sec:settlement}). An invalid or missing compliance proof does not halt loan settlement or affect borrowers; only the operator's fee is at risk (Section~\ref{subsec:feegating}).

\subsection{Fee-Gating}
\label{subsec:feegating}

Operators earn a spread fee escrowed during each epoch. A valid compliance proof releases the escrow; otherwise it flows back into vault NAV as LP uplift. LP-facing yield is not contingent on the compliance proof: LPs always receive their share of interest and buffer surplus.

\subsection{NAV and Shares}

LPs deposit USDC and receive ERC-20 shares. The vault's net asset value is
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{NAV} = U_{\mathrm{idle}} + \sum_{j} v_j \cdot n_j + B_{\mathrm{escrow}} + I_{\mathrm{accrued}} - W_{\mathrm{pending}},
  \label{eq:nav}
\end{equation}
where $U_{\mathrm{idle}}$ is undeployed USDC, $v_j \cdot n_j$ is the claim value (Equation~\ref{eq:debtprice}) times quantity for each held debt token~$j$, $B_{\mathrm{escrow}}$ is escrowed hedging buffers, $I_{\mathrm{accrued}}$ is accrued but uncollected interest, and $W_{\mathrm{pending}}$ is USDC ring-fenced for pending withdrawals. Share price is NAV per outstanding supply, with a virtual offset against inflation attacks. On vault-funded loans, the vault earns the full fixed-rate coupon; on liquidity-forwarding loans, the vault earns buffer surplus. Impairments reduce NAV directly via share-price adjustment.

\subsection{Epoch Withdrawal}
\label{subsec:withdrawal}

Instant withdrawal is incompatible with capital in active positions. The vault queues exits in four steps:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Request.} The LP burns vault shares and receives \emph{withdrawal receipt} tokens for the current epoch.
  \item \textbf{Epoch elapses.} After the epoch duration, anyone can advance the epoch.
  \item \textbf{Settlement.} A permissionless call snapshots the share price for all pending requests in that epoch and ring-fences the corresponding USDC, removing it from the vault's deployable idle balance.
  \item \textbf{Redemption.} The LP burns receipt tokens and receives the ring-fenced USDC, net of a flat redemption fee.
\end{enumerate}

Until settlement, the LP remains exposed to pool gains and losses. The epoch delay prevents withdrawing at a stale NAV before a loss lands.
