\section{The Underwriter}
\label{sec:lp}

An underwriter aggregates LP capital, underwrites position risk, and stands as economic counterparty to borrowers. The operator matches intents, assembles hook chains, and produces settlement proofs within each underwriter's committed policy; lifecycle proofs (accrual, close, enforcement) are independently producible by either counterparty~\cite{daloc-decent2026}. All actions are bounded by a committed policy hash; compliance is enforced at proof time. The underwriter is the canonical lending book: when it has sufficient available capital, origination deploys it directly at the full coupon (Section~\ref{sec:capital}); when demand exceeds capacity, origination uses the liquidity-forwarding hook chain and the underwriter commits only hedging reserves.

\subsection{Operator and Policy}

The operator submits quotes to the order book (Section~\ref{sec:architecture}), competing on terms; on match, keepers execute settlement proofs that deploy underwriter capital within committed policy bounds. The underwriter's strategy is bounded by a \textbf{committed policy}: a hash registered on-chain at deployment. Each policy specifies:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Allowed collateral set:} asset addresses the underwriter may accept as loan collateral.
  \item \textbf{Per-collateral LTV ceiling:} maximum loan-to-value ratio the underwriter may offer on each collateral type.
  \item \textbf{Per-tenor rate floor:} minimum annualized rate the underwriter must charge.
  \item \textbf{Liquidation bonus ranges:} acceptable bonus percentages committed at origination.
  \item \textbf{Portfolio concentration caps:} maximum exposure per borrower, per collateral type, and per market as a fraction of underwriter NAV.
  \item \textbf{Sweep frequency:} maximum interval between health-check sweeps.
  \item \textbf{Minimum buffer ratio:} minimum first-loss buffer maintained at all times relative to total exposure.
\end{itemize}

A policy constrains what the operator \emph{can} do, not what they \emph{must} do; within its bounds, the operator retains full discretion on timing, counterparty selection, and position sizing.

\subsection{Compliance Enforcement}

Every origination proof verifies that the proposed position satisfies the underwriter's committed policy hash. The circuit checks that funded loans, hedging reserves, external venue choices, concentration exposure, rate floors, LTV ceilings, and buffer ratios remain within the policy's bounds. An action that violates the policy cannot produce a valid proof.

The detailed proof format, public inputs, and fact verification flow are part of the settlement-program framework described in Section~\ref{sec:settlement}. The decentralization paper~\cite{daloc-decent2026} specifies how compliance enforcement operates without reliance on the operator.

\subsection{Capital Provision and Shares}

LPs keep funds in their own wallets and grant the underwriter an on-chain allowance. In return, they receive transferable ERC-20 shares representing proportional claims on deployed capital and returns. Funds leave LP wallets only at the instant of proof-gated deployment into a specific position.

Share price reflects the underwriter's net asset value as recorded on-chain:
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{NAV} = \sum_{j} v_j \cdot n_j + B_{\mathrm{buffer}} + I_{\mathrm{proven}},
  \label{eq:nav}
\end{equation}
where $v_j \cdot n_j$ is the claim value (Equation~\ref{eq:debtprice}) times quantity for each held debt token~$j$, $B_{\mathrm{buffer}}$ is the first-loss buffer balance, and $I_{\mathrm{proven}}$ is interest verified by accrual proofs but not yet applied to the corresponding debt tokens' on-chain claim values~$v_j$ (the increment between fact registration and debt-token state update). Because accrual proofs update balances discretely, on-chain NAV conservatively lags mathematically accrued interest between proof submissions; accrual proof producers are compensated with a deferred fraction of accrued interest, incentivizing timely accounting. Share price is NAV per outstanding supply, with a virtual offset against inflation attacks. On direct-funded loans, the underwriter earns the full coupon (fixed or variable, depending on the rate model); on liquidity-forwarding loans, the underwriter earns buffer surplus. Impairments reduce NAV directly via share-price adjustment.

\subsection{First-Loss Buffer}

Protection comes from a first-loss buffer funded primarily by per-origination fees. When a position produces bad debt, the buffer absorbs the shortfall before share price moves. The creation proof enforces a minimum buffer-to-exposure ratio, blocking new originations when the buffer thins. The loss hierarchy:
\begin{enumerate}
  \item borrower repayments and collateral value;
  \item loan-specific funding and execution reserves;
  \item permissionless liquidation;
  \item underwriter first-loss buffer;
  \item residual LP impairment.
\end{enumerate}

One limitation: the on-chain buffer ratio reflects only realized losses (positions that have been enforced and closed at a loss). Positions in bad-debt territory but not yet enforced still appear as healthy exposure, potentially overstating buffer adequacy. Timely enforcement by counterparties mitigates this gap~\cite{daloc-decent2026}.

Because the buffer is funded primarily by origination fees, it faces a procyclical gap: during a downturn, new origination drops precisely when existing positions may be deteriorating, starving the buffer of inflows. To prevent an irrecoverable state where buffer depletion permanently blocks origination, the underwriter contract accepts direct capital injections into the buffer from any source. This injection is voluntary and does not dilute LP shares; it restores origination capacity.

\subsection{LP Exit}

LP exit follows the allowance-and-shares model: revoke the allowance and further deployments from that wallet stop immediately. Shares on already-deployed capital remain outstanding until underlying positions close and capital returns, or the LP sells shares on secondary markets for immediate liquidity. The critical stability property: exit constrains future origination capacity, not existing healthy positions, so withdrawal pressure cannot trigger asset sales or position unwinds~\cite{daloc-decent2026}.
