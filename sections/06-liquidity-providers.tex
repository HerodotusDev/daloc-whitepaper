\section{The Harvest Vault}
\label{sec:lp}

The Harvest Vault's operator originates loans, responds to lifecycle events, and manages portfolio concentration. All actions are bounded by a publicly auditable mandate; compliance is attested per-epoch by a zero-knowledge proof and enforced through fee-gating. The vault is the canonical lending book: when it has sufficient idle capital, it funds loans directly at the full fixed rate (Section~\ref{sec:capital}); when demand exceeds capacity, it routes through external venues and commits only hedging reserves. The mandate, not operator reputation, is the trust anchor for deployed capital.

\subsection{Operator and Mandate}

The operator deploys vault capital by submitting solver quotes to the order book (Section~\ref{sec:architecture}), competing on terms like any other solver. The operator's strategy is bounded by a \textbf{mandate}: a policy committed on-chain as a hash at vault deployment. Each mandate specifies:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Allowed collateral set:} asset addresses the operator may accept as loan collateral.
  \item \textbf{Per-collateral LTV ceiling:} maximum loan-to-value ratio the operator may offer on each collateral type.
  \item \textbf{Per-tenor rate floor:} minimum annualized rate the operator must charge; violation forfeits the epoch fee rather than reverting the loan.
  \item \textbf{Portfolio concentration caps:} maximum exposure per borrower, per collateral type, and per market as a fraction of vault NAV.
  \item \textbf{Liquidation reserve requirement:} minimum idle capital maintained at all times for liquidation response and withdrawal settlement.
\end{itemize}

A mandate constrains what the operator \emph{can} do, not what they \emph{must} do; within its bounds, the operator retains full discretion on timing, counterparty selection, and position sizing.

\subsection{Compliance Proof}

Each epoch, the operator produces a zero-knowledge compliance proof that the vault's actions respected the mandate committed at deployment. At a high level, the proof is generated from the prior vault state, the epoch action log, the resulting vault state, and the mandate hash. It verifies that funded loans, hedging reserves, external venue choices, concentration exposure, rate floors, LTV ceilings, and liquidation reserves remained within the mandate's bounds.

The proof outputs a compliance fact registered in the facts registry and consumed by the fee-gating mechanism (Section~\ref{subsec:feegating}). The detailed proof format, public inputs, and fact verification flow are part of the settlement-program framework described in Section~\ref{sec:settlement}. An invalid or missing compliance proof does not halt loan settlement or affect borrowers; it only prevents the operator from collecting the epoch fee.

\subsection{Fee-Gating}
\label{subsec:feegating}

Operators earn a spread fee escrowed during each epoch. A valid compliance fact for the corresponding vault and epoch releases the escrow; otherwise it flows back into vault NAV as LP uplift. LP-facing yield is not contingent on the compliance proof: LPs always receive their share of interest and buffer surplus.

\subsection{NAV and Shares}

LPs deposit USDC and receive ERC-20 shares. The vault's net asset value is
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{NAV} = U_{\mathrm{idle}} + \sum_{j} v_j \cdot n_j + B_{\mathrm{escrow}} + I_{\mathrm{accrued}} - W_{\mathrm{pending}},
  \label{eq:nav}
\end{equation}
where $U_{\mathrm{idle}}$ is undeployed USDC, $v_j \cdot n_j$ is the claim value (Equation~\ref{eq:debtprice}) times quantity for each held debt token~$j$, $B_{\mathrm{escrow}}$ is escrowed hedging buffers, $I_{\mathrm{accrued}}$ is interest that has elapsed chronologically but not yet been verified by a ZK proof (and thus not yet reflected in $v_j$), and $W_{\mathrm{pending}}$ is USDC ring-fenced for pending withdrawals. Share price is NAV per outstanding supply, with a virtual offset against inflation attacks. On vault-funded loans, the vault earns the full fixed-rate coupon; on liquidity-forwarding loans, the vault earns buffer surplus. Impairments reduce NAV directly via share-price adjustment.

\subsection{Epoch Withdrawal}
\label{subsec:withdrawal}

Instant withdrawal is incompatible with capital in active positions. The vault queues exits in four steps:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Request.} The LP burns vault shares and receives \emph{withdrawal receipt} tokens for the current epoch.
  \item \textbf{Epoch elapses.} After the epoch duration, anyone can advance the epoch.
  \item \textbf{Settlement.} A permissionless call snapshots the share price for all pending requests in that epoch and ring-fences the corresponding USDC, removing it from the vault's deployable idle balance.
  \item \textbf{Redemption.} The LP burns receipt tokens and receives the ring-fenced USDC, net of a flat redemption fee.
\end{enumerate}

Until settlement, the LP's redemption value tracks vault NAV (gains and losses included). The epoch delay prevents withdrawing at a stale NAV before a loss lands.
