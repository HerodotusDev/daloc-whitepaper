\begin{abstract}
  \daloc{} is a cross-chain credit coordination and settlement protocol for prime brokerage and structured lending. A single collateral deposit provides unified margin, leverage, and risk management across multiple chains and venues without bridging the underlying asset. Collateral stays on its home chain; yield-bearing tokens continue accruing native yield. Coordination relies on zero-knowledge storage proofs rather than trusted relayers, while token movement uses existing bridge infrastructure.

  Three products share one protocol, built around independently operated markets creatable permissionlessly from governance-registered program hashes. Borrow originates loans with configurable rate models (fixed or variable) against collateral on any supported chain. Prime Trade opens a credit line from collateral to a perpetuals venue, where borrowed funds are restricted to trading by market-defined margin agents. Earn gives liquidity providers yield from a portfolio of loans: capital is deployed by an underwriter whose strategy is bounded by a committed policy hash enforced at proof time.

  A common order book structure matches borrower intents against underwriter terms within each market; criteria-based filtering lets regulated and open participants share one liquidity surface without permissioned forks. Capital deploys directly from underwriter balances when available; liquidity forwarding extends reach through established lending venues, reserving only hedging capital to cover funding mismatch. LPs keep funds in their own wallets and grant allowances; capital moves only at proof-gated deployment into a specific position. Markets are defined by immutable registered zero-knowledge program hashes and parameters; risk terms are per-loan, dynamically simulated, and zero-knowledge provable. Settlement is event-driven; position terms remain off-chain commitments with only hashes and counters public. Loan positions are tokenized as transferable debt instruments with provably accruing interest.
\end{abstract}
