\begin{abstract}
  \daloc{} is a cross-chain credit coordination and settlement protocol for prime brokerage and structured lending. A single collateral deposit provides unified margin, leverage, and risk management across multiple chains and venues without bridging the underlying asset. Collateral stays on its home chain; yield-bearing tokens continue accruing native yield. Coordination relies on zero-knowledge storage proofs rather than trusted relayers, while token movement uses existing bridge infrastructure.

  Three products share one protocol, built around independently operated markets that anyone can deploy. Borrow originates fixed-rate, fixed-term loans against collateral on any supported chain. Prime Trade opens a credit line from collateral to a perpetuals venue, where borrowed funds are restricted to trading by market-defined margin agents. Earn gives liquidity providers yield from a portfolio of fixed-rate loans: capital is deployed by a vault operator whose strategy is bounded by a proof-attested mandate enforced through fee-gating.

  A common order book structure matches borrower intents against solver quotes within each market; criteria-based filtering lets regulated and open participants share one liquidity surface without permissioned forks. Capital is sourced through liquidity forwarding: solvers borrow from established lending venues against pledged collateral, reserving only hedging capital to convert variable funding to fixed terms; the protocol can originate loans before accumulating LP deposits. Markets are defined by permissionless zero-knowledge programs, and risk terms are per-loan, dynamically simulated, and zero-knowledge provable. Loan positions are tokenized as transferable debt instruments with provably accruing interest.
\end{abstract}
