\section*{Abstract}

\daloc{} is a cross-chain prime brokerage and lending platform. A single collateral deposit provides unified margin, leverage, and risk management across multiple chains and venues. Collateral stays on its home chain and continues accruing native yield; coordination relies on zero-knowledge and storage proofs rather than trusted bridges or relayers.

Three products share one platform. \textbf{Borrow} originates fixed-rate, fixed-term loans against collateral on any supported chain. \textbf{Prime Trade} opens a credit line from yield-bearing collateral to a perpetuals venue---borrowed funds are restricted to trading by protocol-controlled margin agents. \textbf{Earn} gives liquidity providers passive fixed-rate yield: capital is deployed into loans by a vault operator whose strategy is bounded by a proof-enforced mandate.

A unified order book matches borrower intents against solver quotes, settling at the best available rate. No capital is locked before settlement, and criteria-based filtering lets regulated and open participants share one liquidity surface. Each loan executes as an isolated position within a single EVM transaction.

Solvers deploy collateral on established lending protocols rather than a proprietary pool, reserving only hedging capital to convert variable funding to fixed terms---achieving 15--30$\times$ capital leverage and eliminating the cold-start problem from the first loan. Markets are defined by permissionless zero-knowledge programs that govern accrual, liquidation, and collateral policy. Risk is quantified dynamically through simulation, producing per-loan terms rather than static protocol parameters. Loan positions are tokenised as transferable debt instruments with provably accruing interest, giving creditors a liquid, tradeable claim.
