\section*{Abstract}

\daloc{} is a multichain lending platform that enables leveraged positions across multiple chains and venues through a single collateral deposit. Its Harvest Vault deploys capital into fixed-rate loans under a cryptographically-enforced mandate: when vault liquidity is sufficient, the operator funds loan principals directly; when demand exceeds capacity, the platform extends its reach through external lending venues, reserving vault capital only for the hedging instruments that convert variable-rate funding to fixed terms. The platform is organized around a unified order book in which borrower requests and solver quotes are symmetric objects---matched through a single pipeline that evaluates both economic terms and criteria-based counterparty compatibility, enabling restricted and unrestricted participants to share one liquidity surface without permissioned forks. Each loan creates an isolated position whose collateral, funding, and external interactions are composed from atomic hook chains: multi-step pipelines---deposit, supply, borrow, bridge---executed within a single transaction on the EVM. Markets are defined by zero-knowledge programs that govern interest accrual, liquidation, and collateral policy---from classical locked-collateral designs to more capital-efficient strategies that permit managed rehypothecation---with all logic publicly auditable and cryptographically enforced. The vault operator's compliance with its mandate is verified per-epoch through the same zero-knowledge proof pipeline, with performance fees released only upon valid attestation. Risk is quantified through on-chain data collection, statistical modeling, and Monte Carlo simulation, producing dynamic per-loan terms rather than static protocol parameters. Loans can be refinanced through the order book---solvers propose new terms, and the existing position is rolled atomically within a single epoch transition. Loan positions are represented as transferable debt tokens with linearly accruing interest, giving creditors a liquid, tradeable claim whose secondary-market price reflects real-time credit risk. The result is a prime brokerage platform that manages positions, provides leverage, and calculates risk across chains and venues through a single interface.
