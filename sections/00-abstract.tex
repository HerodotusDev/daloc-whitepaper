\section*{Abstract}

\daloc{} is a multichain lending platform that enables leveraged positions across multiple chains and venues through a single collateral deposit. Its Harvest Vault deploys capital into fixed-rate loans under a cryptographically-enforced mandate, funding principal directly when liquidity permits or routing through external lending venues while reserving only hedging capital. A unified order book matches borrower requests and solver quotes through a single pipeline that evaluates both economic terms and criteria-based counterparty compatibility, allowing restricted and unrestricted participants to share one liquidity surface. Each loan creates an isolated position whose collateral, funding, and external interactions are composed from atomic hook chains executed within a single EVM transaction. Markets are defined by zero-knowledge programs governing interest accrual, liquidation, and collateral policy, with all logic publicly auditable. Risk is quantified through on-chain data collection, statistical modeling, and Monte Carlo simulation, producing dynamic per-loan terms. Loan positions are represented as transferable debt tokens with linearly accruing interest, giving creditors a tradeable claim whose secondary-market price reflects real-time credit risk.
