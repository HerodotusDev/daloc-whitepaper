\section*{Abstract}

\daloc{} is a multichain lending platform that enables leveraged positions across multiple chains and venues through a single collateral deposit. It is organized around a unified order book in which borrower requests and solver quotes are symmetric objects---a request-for-quote populates the same book as a standing offer, ensuring a single matching and settlement pipeline regardless of how liquidity arrives. Each loan creates an isolated position whose collateral, funding, and external interactions are composed from atomic hook chains: multi-step pipelines---deposit, supply, borrow, bridge---executed within a single transaction on the EVM. Solvers deploy pledged collateral on established lending protocols, sourcing liquidity without bootstrapping a proprietary pool; the platform reserves only a small fraction of capital to underwrite the hedging instruments that convert variable-rate funding to fixed terms, solving the cold-start problem from the first loan originated. Markets are defined by zero-knowledge programs that govern interest accrual, liquidation, and collateral policy---from classical locked-collateral designs to more capital-efficient strategies that permit managed rehypothecation---with all logic publicly auditable and cryptographically enforced. Risk is quantified through on-chain data collection, statistical modeling, and Monte Carlo simulation, producing dynamic per-loan terms rather than static protocol parameters. Loan positions are represented as transferable debt tokens with provably accruing interest, giving creditors a liquid, tradeable claim. The result is a prime brokerage platform that manages positions, provides leverage, and calculates risk across chains and venues through a single interface.
