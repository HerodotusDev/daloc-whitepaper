\section{Cross-Chain Exposure}
\label{sec:crosschain}

Cross-chain operation is the default; same-chain loans are a special case. Two concerns arise at different layers.

\textbf{Authorization} (protocol-level): proving that collateral was pledged on the collateral chain so the principal chain's contracts can act on it. \daloc{} uses ZK storage proofs via the Herodotus Data Processor (HDP)~\cite{hdp}, combining storage proofs with MMR-backed chain history into a single verifiable claim.

\textbf{Token movement} (solver-level): physically moving the borrowed asset to its destination. Solvers choose the bridge path; for USDC, Circle's CCTP~\cite{cctp} is the common choice. Bridge hooks compose movement into the origination hook chain.

\subsection{Deterministic Addressing}

The hub deploys at the same address on every supported chain. A loan can lock collateral on chain~A while the issued debt token lives on chain~B, coordinated via shared epoch commitments and deterministic \texttt{CREATE2} addresses. New destinations require only a bridge adapter whitelisted on the hub.

\subsection{Hyperliquid Margin Agent}

The Hyperliquid margin pipeline (Section~\ref{sec:lifecycle}) routes borrowed USDC via CCTP to a per-loan \emph{margin agent} on HyperEVM at a \texttt{CREATE2} address computed at quote time. The margin agent deposits USDC into HyperCore as margin---capital never enters the borrower's wallet. Permitted actions are restricted to perpetual trading; withdrawals, spot transfers, and external lending are blocked.

On close or liquidation, the margin agent recalls capital from HyperCore and burns it via CCTP back to Ethereum. Collateral never crosses to HyperEVM; only USDC round-trips. Figure~\ref{fig:primetrade} illustrates the full originate--active--close cycle.

\subsection{Effective Leverage}

The externally-sourced path creates a leverage multiplier: a borrower depositing 40~ETH (\$80{,}000 at \$2{,}000/ETH) borrows \$50{,}000 USDC at 62.5\% LTV, bridged to a margin agent on Hyperliquid for up to 5$\times$ venue leverage, controlling \$250{,}000 notional --- 3.1$\times$ effective leverage without selling the underlying. The lender remains fully collateralized on Ethereum throughout.
